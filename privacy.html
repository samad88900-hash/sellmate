Privacy Policy

SellMate · Last updated: 25 November 2026

SellMate is a seller workspace for products, orders, customers, payments, expenses and chat drafts. This policy explains what data is stored and how it is handled.

Accounts

You can sign in with an email and password or with Google. Supabase handles authentication. We never receive or store your Google password. Google sign-in only provides your basic profile information such as name, email address and profile picture.

Your shop data

Products, orders, customers, payments, expenses, suppliers, chats and settings are stored in Supabase under your account. Row Level Security (RLS) makes sure only you can read and change your own workspace. Your data is not sold or shared with other users.

Device storage

The app may cache the interface in your browser so it can load offline. Business data is kept in your cloud workspace, not shared between accounts.

Third parties

We use Supabase for authentication and data storage, Netlify for hosting, and Google for optional sign-in. These services process data according to their own policies.

Your choices

You can change your password inside the app, request a password reset by email, download a backup, or permanently delete your account and all its data from the account menu. We also remove workspace data when an account is deleted.
